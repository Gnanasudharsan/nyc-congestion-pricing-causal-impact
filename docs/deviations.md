# Deviations from the pre-registered analysis plan

The plan is tagged `analysis-plan-v1` (Phase 1). Every change after that tag is logged here.
No treatment-effect estimate existed when any of the entries below were made.

| # | Date | Section of plan | Change | Reason | Before or after seeing related estimates? |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 2026-11-09 | §10 Data handling (holidays) | **NYC public-school closure indicators are not built in Phase 2.** Federal and NY State holiday indicators, and holiday-week flags, are built. | No machine-readable NYC DOE calendar was found on NYC Open Data. School closures enter only one robustness check. Phase 5 will either hand-code closure dates from the published DOE calendars (with source links, committed as data) or drop the check and say so here. | Before (no estimates exist) |
| 2 | 2026-11-09 | §8 Decision rules (MDEs) | For the synthetic-control outcomes (R1, A1), the Phase 2 MDE uses an **equal-weight donor average** as a proxy for the fitted synthetic control. DiD outcomes use the pre-registered specification. | The synthetic-control estimator is implemented and unit-tested in Phase 3. Fitted weights track the treated unit at least as well as equal weights, so the proxy MDE is expected to be conservative (too large). MDEs will be recomputed with the fitted estimator in Phase 3 and both versions reported. | Before |
| 3 | 2026-11-09 | §4.3 T3/S4 speeds | **A zone-day enters the speed panel only if it has ≥ 20 speed-valid trips** in peak hours within its own group. | Unspecified in the plan. Zone-days with very few internal trips produce extremely noisy speeds. Phase 5 adds a robustness check with no minimum and observations weighted by trip count. | Before |
| 4 | 2026-11-09 | §5.2 A1/A3 (smoke-day rule) | "Medium or heavy HMS smoke covering New York County" is implemented as any Medium/Heavy HMS polygon **intersecting** the union of Manhattan's taxi-zone polygons. | Makes the plan's wording operational. "Covering" could mean full coverage or any overlap; we use any overlap, the more conservative choice, which excludes more days. | Before |
| 5 | 2026-11-09 | §7, §8, new §11 (amendment 1, tag `analysis-plan-v1.1`) | **Adds a pre-period-detrended secondary specification** for every DiD and synthetic-control outcome. For the flagged outcomes (T2, R4, A1, A3, R2), "robust" also requires the detrended estimate to match the primary estimate's sign, with a CI that excludes zero. The primary specification is unchanged. | The Phase 2 pre-period screen showed the treated-minus-control gap drifting for these outcomes. The amendment applies to all outcomes, so outcomes are not chosen by eye, and it only adds a condition for a claim, never removes one ([ADR 0011](adr/0011-detrended-secondary-specification.md)). | Before (no estimates exist; based on pre-period descriptives only) |
| 6 | 2026-11-09 | §4.1 event-study specification | **Event study uses calendar 2023 as the seasonal baseline.** Bin indicators cover 2024 pre-period bins, anticipation, and post bins, and coefficients are expressed relative to the reference window ([ADR 0012](adr/0012-event-study-seasonal-baseline.md)). The pooled DiD (the headline) is unchanged. | Tested on simulated data with a known effect: as written, the treated seasonal profile is nearly collinear with 28-day bins (13 bins = 364 days), and bins pick up seasonal noise that looks like pre-trends. The revised specification recovers the true path. | Before (no estimates exist) |
| 7 | 2026-11-09 | ADR 0006 (HonestDiD implementation) | **The `honestdid` 0.1.1 port runs in its own locked environment** (`scripts/honestdid_bounds.py`, NumPy 1.26), called from the project through JSON files. | Validated: it reproduces all 20 published R interval endpoints (relative magnitudes and smoothness) to 2 dp (`results/honestdid_validation.csv`). Running it in isolation keeps the validated code unchanged, rather than patching it or re-implementing it. | Before (no estimates exist) |
| 8 | 2026-11-09 | §5.2 A1 donor pool | **Missing donor-weeks are imputed with donor + week effects. Weeks where fewer than 75% of donors report are dropped.** The treated series is never imputed. A1/A4 use 121 weeks and impute 3.4% of donor cells. | The plan did not say how to handle gaps in donor series. Synthetic control needs a balanced donor matrix, and only 9 of 99 donors report in every week where the treated sites are valid. The coverage rule stops invention of long stretches: AQS donor files end in mid-2026. As a consequence, **A1 has no extended-window (2026) estimate**. A Phase 5 check re-estimates with donors ≥ 98% complete. | Before (no estimates exist) |
| 9 | 2026-11-09 | §7 check 1 (pre-trend test) | **The cluster-robust joint Wald pre-trend test is used only with ≥ 30 clusters.** With fewer (T1: 7 facilities; A3: 4 sites), the test is randomization inference on the sum of squared pre-period bins. | On simulated data with 16 clusters, the Wald test rejected a true null with F = 14.6. ADR 0005 already sets 30 clusters as the threshold for cluster-robust inference. | Before (no estimates exist) |
| 10 | 2026-11-09 | §4–5 implementation details | (a) TLC zone-days with no trips are filled as zeros: the outcome is log(1 + trips), and 839 of about 127k control zone-days are affected. (b) R2: control complexes with any station whose daytime routes include the G train are dropped from 2024-06-28 to 2024-09-03 (the handling registered in confounders.md). (c) The extended window (daily) drops 2026-01-01 to 01-03, so it starts at the 2026-01-04 fare and toll change. Weekly periods are classified by whole-week membership (`designs/windows.py`). (d) Phase 3 scope: the primary outcomes plus the non-spillover secondaries with Phase 2 panels (T5, R2, R3, R4, A3, A4). Spillover secondaries are Phase 4. BH q-values are computed in Phase 5 once each family is complete. (e) The Pandey et al. replication uses weekdays in weeks 1–14 under both ISO and calendar-week conventions (the paper does not say which), and excludes Governor's, Ellis, and Liberty islands from the 'north of 60th St' controls. (f) Synthetic controls report a placebo-effect range (2.5th–97.5th percentile), labelled as such, alongside the rank p-value. | Each is unspecified in the plan or an operational reading of it. None depends on outcome data after the treatment date. | Before (no estimates exist) |
| 11 | 2026-11-09 | §5.2 A3 weather covariates | **The primary A3 weather adjustment omits boundary-layer height (BLH).** It keeps temperature, humidity, wind speed, precipitation, and wind sector. Requiring BLH would drop 26% of the pre-period. Found when the A3 fit failed to converge, before any A3 estimate existed. | Before (no A3 estimate existed) |
| 12 | 2026-11-09 | §9 equity data (ACS source) | **ACS 2019–2023 5-year tract data come from the Census Bureau's table-based summary files** (keyless public downloads), not the Census API. | The API now redirects every request to a 'missing key' page (checked 2026-11-09). A key requires an account, and the plan assumed none was needed. The tables and vintage are the same. | Before (no Phase 4 estimate existed) |
| 13 | 2026-11-09 | §9 equity data (asthma) | **Asthma measure: adult ED visits, average annual age-adjusted rate per 10,000, by NTA 2010, 2017–2019** (EH Data Portal indicator 2384, measure 1209). | 14 | 2026-11-09 | §9 equity (geography) | **Tract and NTA values are allocated to taxi zones by overlap area** (EPSG:2263). | 15 | 2026-11-09 | §9 equity (zone effects) | **Zone-level S1/S3 effects come from one regression with a treated x post (and anticipation) indicator per spillover zone**, sharing the far-control group. Each zone's sampling SD is the SD of its estimates at the 14 pre-period placebo dates (MDE dates). Shrinkage uses DerSimonian–Laird normal-normal empirical Bayes. The associations use population-weighted correlations, and population-weighted bivariate and multivariable regressions on standardised covariates with HC1 SEs. | With a single treated cluster per coefficient, cluster-robust SEs are not reliable. The placebo-in-time SD measures each zone's own noise. The plan left the shrinkage method and the noise estimate unspecified. Exploratory: no causal interpretation. | Before (no Phase 4 estimate existed) |
| 16 | 2026-11-09 | §9 heterogeneity cuts | **Each cut is estimated as a stacked regression with level-specific unit x season and period fixed effects** (equivalent to separate regressions), clustered by the original unit. Each level's effect and the level-minus-reference difference are reported. BH is applied within each cut to the difference tests (borough: to the borough effects). Cuts with fewer than 30 clusters (T1) use wild-cluster-bootstrap p-values. | The plan listed the cuts but not the estimator, outcomes, or test family. A stacked design gives a valid SE for the difference. | Before (no Phase 4 estimate existed) |
| 17 | 2026-11-09 | §3.2/§5.3 spillover family | **S2 uses randomization inference over all C(8,3) = 56 assignments** (8 facilities, fewer than 30 clusters; ADR 0005), with the wild bootstrap as a check. **A2 enters the spillover BH family through design (a), synthetic control.** Design (b), the weather-adjusted DiD (Bronx sites vs the Queens College NO2 pair, randomization inference over 6 assignments), is reported as A2b without entering the family. | 18 | 2026-11-09 | §7 falsification checks (pass criteria) | **Pass criteria, fixed before any Phase 5 check was run.** (a) *Placebo date* (2024-01-07, data before 2024-11-14) and *placebo outcomes* (Staten Island trips vs the other far-control zones; Verrazzano-Narrows vs the other four control facilities): pass if the 95% interval includes zero **or** the estimate is less than half the primary estimate in magnitude. (b) *Re-estimation checks*: pass if the sign matches the primary estimate. These are control-group swaps for T2, S1, and T3 (Brooklyn-only far control, Queens-only far control, intermediate band), the June 2024 pause dropped (2024-05-15 to 07-15), holiday weeks dropped, the did2s imputation estimator, and leave-one-out donors. (c) *Dose-response*: pass if |overnight| ≤ |peak|. Applies only to T1, T3, and S2, where the toll's 75% overnight discount changes the dose; not applicable to TLC trip counts, whose per-trip fee is the same at all hours. (d) *HonestDiD*: relative magnitudes on the 2025 average of the event-study post bins (l = 1/13 each). Pre-period = 2024 bins relative to the reference window; the anticipation coefficient is dropped from β and Σ; M̄ ∈ {0.25, 0.5, 0.75, 1, 1.5, 2}. Breakdown M̄ is reported on that grid. Not computed when the cluster-robust Σ is singular (fewer than 30 clusters: T1, S2, A3, A2b). (e) *Smoothness (amendment 1) for T2, R2* (A3 has 4 clusters, so it is not computed): M ∈ {0, 0.5, 1, 2} × the largest absolute second difference of the pre-period bins. | The plan named the checks but not numeric pass rules. Fixing them in advance avoids judging checks after seeing them. Staten Island 'entirely within' is approximated by SI pickups with drop-offs outside the CRZ, because the panel records drop-off group, not borough. | Before (no Phase 5 check had been run) |
